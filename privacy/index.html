Privacy

Last updated October 27, 2026

Bappy is software for merchant cash advance shops. It texts the shop's leads, holds the conversations, brings in applications and documents, and helps the shop's brokers get deals funded. This page explains what information Bappy handles, why, and the choices you have. Questions go to mendy@bappy.ai.

Who this covers

  • Shops and their team (owners and brokers) who sign in to Bappy.
  • Merchants a shop works with: the businesses Bappy texts and emails for the shop.

A shop decides which merchants Bappy contacts and owns its merchant relationships. Bappy works on the shop's behalf.

What Bappy collects

  • Team details: name, work email, phone number, and sign-in records.
  • Merchant details the shop provides: business and contact names, phone numbers and emails from the shop's lead lists.
  • Conversations: the texts and emails between Bappy, the shop's brokers and merchants.
  • Applications and documents a merchant sends the shop, such as an application form and bank statements.
  • Google account details when a broker connects Gmail (see below).

How Bappy uses it

Only to do the shop's work: texting and replying to merchants, following up, collecting applications and documents, reading them so brokers can act, and showing the shop its own results. Bappy does not sell personal information and does not use it for advertising. Bappy never asks merchants for bank logins or Social Security numbers by text.

Gmail and other Google data

When a broker connects Gmail, Bappy asks Google for two things: the broker's email address, and permission to send email on the broker's behalf. Bappy uses that permission only to send the emails the broker's shop has set Bappy up to send, such as an application link to a merchant who asked for one, from the broker's own address. Bappy does not read the broker's mailbox with it.

Bappy's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Bappy does not use Google user data to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models, does not sell it, and does not let people read it except with the user's consent, for security, or where the law requires.

A broker can disconnect at any time in Bappy's Settings, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

If a shop connects its own shared submissions mailbox, Bappy reads the funder emails in it to keep each of the shop's deals up to date, and shows them only to that shop.

Who else sees it

Each shop sees only its own information. Bappy shares information only with the service providers that help run it (cloud hosting, texting, email and AI model providers), only so they can provide that service, or when the law requires it.

Texts

A merchant can reply STOP to any text to stop receiving texts from that shop's Bappy number. Bappy honors it right away.

Bappy also texts from its own number, only to business owners who ask for a Bappy demo. The texting terms explain those texts, including how to stop them.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

Keeping it safe and how long

Bappy uses encrypted connections and limits who on our team can see shop information. A shop can also turn off our team's access entirely. Information is kept while the shop uses Bappy and deleted when the shop asks after it leaves, unless the law requires keeping it longer.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete your information, write to mendy@bappy.ai. If you are a merchant, you can also ask the shop that contacted you.
